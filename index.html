Mass accounting (conceptual)

Feed inAir + H₂O + CO₂ + trace
Retained on bedH₂O + CO₂ (adsorbed phase)
Purified feed outAir, depleted in H₂O / CO₂
Regeneration outPurge + desorbed H₂O / CO₂

Nothing disappears. What the bed holds in one state, it releases in another. That accounting is the whole story.
